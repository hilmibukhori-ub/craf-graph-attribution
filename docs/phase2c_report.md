## 5. Small-budget feasibility (task 4)

* With `n_min = 5`, the adaptive branch is infeasible at b = 6 for K18 and K48. The Phase 2B rule
  then makes no claim, and loses significantly to exhaustive: −0.300 [−0.417, −0.183] in
  S8_large_hard K18, −0.367 in S8_large_easy.
* **The a-priori fallback (`_fb`) removes that loss entirely.** It is identical to exhaustive at
  b = 6 by construction (P7), and validity is unchanged.
* `n_min = 3` (`_s`) makes the adaptive branch feasible at b = 6 (100% of experiments), and the
  marginal t-test remains calibrated at n = 3:

| exact-null contrasts, t-test size at alpha 0.05 | n = 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 8 | 12 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SGC S7_hard (groups: 7200 … 720) | 0.046 | 0.046 | 0.044 | 0.043 | 0.044 | 0.049 | 0.050 |
| SGC S7_easy | 0.049 | 0.043 | 0.047 | 0.043 | 0.041 | 0.047 | 0.044 |
| SAGE S7_hard (3600 … 360) | 0.044 | 0.040 | 0.044 | 0.053 | 0.048 | 0.053 | 0.047 |

  The exact-null contrasts are symmetric (skew 0.006–0.054) and moderately heavy-tailed (excess
  kurtosis 1.1–2.7). The t-test is slightly conservative, with no anti-conservatism beyond MC
  error.
* **But `_s` does not buy power at b = 6.**
  * `_s − _fb` at b = 6 is −0.083 to +0.100, and no SGC CI excludes 0.
  * For SAGE S8_small_hard b6 it is significantly **worse**: −0.083 [−0.154, −0.013] vs
    exhaustive.
  * With 2–3 stage-2 df and R1 = 2, the adaptive branch has too little information to beat
    spending the whole budget on a single stage.
* **Conclusion.** Small-budget feasibility is solved by the fallback, not by lowering `n_min`.
  Lowering `n_min` is valid but useless or harmful at b = 6.

## 7. Neural replication (GraphSAGE, K18; same instances and placebos as SGC)

* **Truths.** `theta_full` = 0 (S7_hard), 0.0085 (S8_small_hard), 0.0118 (S8_large_hard). The
  binding null is isolated, with a gap of 0.011–0.014.
* **Validity.**
  * Full-set procedures: 0/60 claims in S7_hard at every budget (eps = 0.005).
  * Screens: up to 0.10 (uniform) and 0.10 (random) full-set false claims; 0 against their own
    estimand.
  * PCGA-L: 100%, correct for its own estimand.
* **Power, `_fb − exhaustive`.**
  * S8_small_hard: b12 +0.267 [0.136, 0.397]; b24 +0.417 [0.283, 0.551].
  * S8_large_hard: b9 +0.150 [0.038, 0.262]; b12 +0.233 [0.099, 0.368]; b24 +0.283 [0.168, 0.398].
* **Small budgets.** As with SGC, `_s` is worse at b = 6 (−0.083 [−0.154, −0.013],
  S8_small_hard).
* **Cost.** GraphSAGE costs about 10× SGC per experiment (≈ 20–23 s vs ≈ 2 s of model fitting
  per experiment). The relative comparisons are unchanged.
* **Boundary.** GCN (development seed only) learned no edge-specific effect in S8
  (binding Delta ≈ 0). RUSA certifies relational value **for a given model**. A null result with
  one architecture is not a statement about the graph.

comparator, decoys included, and validity never depends on it.

## 9. Decision

Pre-specified criteria (protocol §5):

| Criterion | Result |
|---|---|
| no full-set validity flag (SGC, SAGE) | **met** (0 false claims at eps = 0.005; nominal at eps = 0) |
| no combination-audit flag | **met** |
| the pre-specified framework procedure is never significantly worse than exhaustive | **not met** for `fullset_adaptive_s`: SAGE S8_small_hard b6, −0.083 [−0.154, −0.013]. It is met for `fullset_adaptive_fb` in every cell, but `_fb` is chosen **after** seeing results, which is disclosed |
| better than exhaustive in at least one S8 hard cell (SGC) | **met** (`_s` and `_fb`, several cells) |
| S8 effect detectable with SAGE | **met** |

→ **CONDITIONAL GO** for real-world experiments, under these conditions:

1. **Framework defaults** (also in `docs/rusa_framework.md`). The full-set certificate is
   `exhaustive_iut`. The optional compute-saving execution mode is `fullset_adaptive_fb`:
   * `n_min = 5`, a-priori fallback, alpha1 = 0.01, `w1² = 1/3`.

   This choice was made after the results and must be stated as such. Because `_fb` equals
   exhaustive whenever it is infeasible, its validity does not depend on that choice; only its
   power does.
2. **Scope labelling is mandatory.**
   * Selected-set screens may be reported only as selected-set or exploratory results. In S7 they
     produced up to 21–24% full-set false claims.
   * PCGA-L results are reported as PCGA-L edge-step results.
3. **Successive halving stays out of the framework**, under the pre-specified rule (it needed
   gains in both discovery and power). It has **no** S8 claim-rate gain over uniform in any cell.

   New evidence it does have: higher discovery in hard K48 cells at small budgets (S7_hard K48 b6
   +0.208 [0.122, 0.295], b12 +0.150 [0.078, 0.222]; S8_large_hard K48 b12 +0.200 [0.079, 0.321]).
   It also has fewer selected-set full-set false claims there (−0.100 [−0.171, −0.029] at K48 b6).

   This matters only for selected-set use, which the framework does not rely on. SH may be named
   as an optional screening heuristic for exploratory reports. It is **not** a contribution.
4. **Real-data analyses are conditional (single-graph) analyses**, which have a different
   estimand from these population studies (§1 of the spec). Replicates must be fresh seed bundles
   with fresh placebo draws (C2), and the t-test calibration must be re-checked with an A/A
   construction on the real graph's own nulls before confirmation.
5. **Model dependence.** The certificate is about a stated model (SGC-ridge, GraphSAGE). Report
   at least one model known to use edge-level information. A GCN null result is uninformative
   here.
6. **Declared candidate sets only.** The guarantee covers the enumerated, audited `J`. A null
   outside `J` (here: stratifying on `u` itself) is not covered.

## 10. Focused literature review (task 8)

| Area | Closest work | Relation to RUSA |
|---|---|---|
| Graph attribution / does the graph help | Hussain et al. 2020 (configuration-model ablation of community structure); Bechler-Speicher et al. 2024 (GNNs use graphs when they shouldn't); Platonov et al. 2023 (heterophily benchmarks); Faber et al. 2021 (pitfalls of ground-truth attribution evaluation) | They use single, fixed null graphs or ablations and give no full-set certificate. RUSA adds a declared family, an admissibility audit and an IUT over the whole family |
| Rewiring controls | Campagne et al. 2026 (degree-preserving double-edge swaps to separate neighbourhood access from neighbour identity); Tortorella & Micheli (rewiring rarely helps); Fosdick et al. 2018 (choosing the right configuration-model space); Orsini et al. 2015 (null-model hierarchy) | They are the source of RUSA's null family. RUSA's addition is certification against the minimum over a family, not a new rewiring |
| Multiverse / specification curves | Steegen et al. 2016; Simonsohn et al. 2020 (joint inference across specifications); Bell et al. 2022 (ML multiverse with GP surrogate) | These are conceptually closest to "robust to every admissible null". Specification-curve joint inference asks whether an effect exists in *some or many* specifications. RUSA's IUT asks whether it holds against *every* admissible null, with a margin. It is a worst-case, not an aggregate, robustness statement |
| Adaptive inference | Bauer & Köhne 1994; Lehmacher & Wassmer 1999; Brannath, Posch & Bauer 2002; Bretz et al. 2009; Benjamini & Heller 2008 (partial conjunction) | `fullset_adaptive` is a direct application, attributed as such. The selected-set vs full-set distinction mirrors adaptive treatment-selection designs, where testing only the selected arms needs closed testing for familywise statements |
| Search / bandits | Jamieson & Talwalkar 2016; Li et al. 2018 (Hyperband) | SH is standard and offers no inferential value here |

No prior work combines all three elements: declared, admissibility-audited graph-null families;
an intersection–union certificate of relational advantage over the *whole* family at a margin;
and explicit selected-set vs full-set scoping under matched compute. The review was focused
rather than systematic, so this is not a proof that no such work exists.

## 12. Limitations

* Synthetic, population-mode evidence only. n = 300 nodes, one metric (1 − Brier), two models.
* `eps = 0.005` and the effect sizes were set by development calibration (disclosed).
* The choice of `_fb` over `_s` as default is post hoc (§9).
* With 60 experiments per S8 cell, power differences below about 0.12 cannot be resolved.
* The t-test's validity is approximate. It is measured here, but real data could have heavier
  tails, so it must be re-audited per dataset.
